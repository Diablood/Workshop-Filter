[h1]Find the recipe you want to craft[/h1]
Workshop Filter adds filters to your workshop recipe list so large mod lists are easier to navigate. The filters apply to final products, not ingredients or bills already in progress.

[h2]Filters that work together[/h2]
[list]
[*]Source mod: show products from a specific mod, Core or DLC.
[*]Apparel layer: select vanilla or custom layers discovered from the workshop's recipes. This control only appears when the workshop can produce apparel.
[*]Nudist compatible: one checkbox using RimWorld's nudity rule, including eligible headgear and accessories. Unchecked applies no nudity restriction.
[*]All active criteria must match the same final product, including recipes with multiple outputs.
[/list]

[h2]Made for everyday use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Search mod and layer lists by name or identifier, ignoring case and accents.
[*]Filters are saved separately for each workshop and restored when you load your game.
[*]Hover over the button to see the active criteria.
[*]Reset in one click, or close the window by clicking outside it.
[*]English and French interface.
[/list]

[h2]Dubs Mint Menus support[/h2]
Works with the native Add bill menu and the recipe panels of Dubs Mint Menus, including its multi-workshop window. Dubs Mint Menus is optional: the mod works without it. When its recipe panel is visible, only the Dubs filter button is displayed.

[h2]Requirements and compatibility[/h2]
RimWorld 1.7 and Harmony are required. Core and DLC are shown as separate product sources. No DLC is required by Workshop Filter itself.

When updating from 0.3.0, enable Workshop Filter again if RimWorld requests it: 0.3.1 corrects its package identity to match the creator's name, Diablood.

Other mods replacing the bill interface may need an adapter. Recipes that generate outputs only through a custom worker without declared recipe products cannot be classified. Filter choices affect only the displayed recipe list; they do not change crafting requirements, ingredient filters or existing bills.

[h2]Saves and feedback[/h2]
Existing saves start without filters. New selections are stored in the save. A removed source mod or apparel layer has its corresponding criterion cleared on load. Version 0.3.0 received user-reported in-game validation; full-game removal and downgrading have not received dedicated tests.

Please include game/mod versions, the workshop, selected filters, relevant interface mods, steps and a relevant log excerpt in bug reports.
